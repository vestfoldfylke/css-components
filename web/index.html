About

A simple css-style generator, only CSS. The goal is to keep it simple, by using native HTML-elements and only CSS, to achieve accessibility and little overhead when developing for the web.

Everything here happens in your browser (so, ironically, you need javascript enabled here), nothing is done server-side, and nothing is sent to the server.

Use in your own project

Generate CSS-file

To use this in your own project - download, insert in your own project and link to the css. You can also customize your own theme, or choose one of the predefined. You can also use the npm-package to generate your own css on the fly.
